Before your visit

Everything you need to walk in prepared

No surprises, no runaround. Here's what to expect, how billing works, how we protect your privacy, and the forms to have ready.

What to expect

Your visit, step by step

1

Book

Call or send the contact form. We confirm the right exam, location, and what you'll need to bring.

2

Prepare

We collect your records and any referral questions in advance so your appointment stays focused.

3

Examine

A clinician conducts a thorough, unhurried exam and documents objective findings.

4

Report

You or the requesting party receive a clear, defensible report — typically within a few business days.

Insurance & payment

Coverage depends on the type of visit — and we'll always tell you the cost before you commit.

  • General & preventive care is billed to most major insurers.
  • IME & many C&P exams are usually paid by the requesting party (attorney, insurer, employer, or the VA).
  • Self-pay options and transparent quotes are available on request.

Specific accepted plans will be listed here once confirmed with the client.

Your privacy (HIPAA)

Patriot Care Center has been HIPAA-compliant since 2023. Protecting your health information isn't a checkbox for us — it's part of the respect we owe you.

  • Records are stored securely and access is limited to your care team.
  • We share your information only with your written authorization or as required by law.
  • You may request a copy of your records at any time.

Full Notice of Privacy Practices available at each location and on request.
