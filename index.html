Who we serve

Built around the people who protect the rest of us

We don't treat your appointment like a form to process. Whether you're filing a VA claim, clearing a department physical, or simply overdue for care, you're seen by clinicians who know your world.

  • Veterans — service-connected evaluations, C&P exams, and continuing care.
  • Firefighters & EMS — annual physicals, return-to-duty, and exposure screening.
  • Government & public-safety personnel — pre-placement and fitness-for-duty exams.
  • Families — general and preventive care for the people you come home to.
