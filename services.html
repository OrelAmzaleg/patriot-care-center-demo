Independent Medical Examinations (IME)

An objective, third-party medical evaluation requested by attorneys, insurers, employers, or courts. Because we hold no stake in the outcome, our findings carry the weight that only genuine independence can.

Who it's for

  • Attorneys building or defending a disability, injury, or workers'-comp case
  • Insurers and employers seeking an unbiased second opinion
  • Veterans who want an independent record alongside their claim

What to bring

  • Government-issued photo ID
  • Relevant medical records, imaging, and prior reports
  • The referral letter or list of questions to be addressed

At a glance

What it is
Independent, unbiased evaluation
Typical length
45–90 minutes
Report turnaround
3–7 business days
An IME is an evaluation, not treatment. We won't establish a care relationship unless you ask us to.

VA Compensation & Pension (C&P) Exams

The examination the VA relies on to rate a service-connected condition. We complete C&P exams on Disability Benefits Questionnaire (DBQ) standards — capturing the history, severity, and functional impact that decide a claim.

Who it's for

  • Veterans with a pending or appealed disability claim
  • Veterans seeking a nexus letter or independent medical opinion
  • Representatives (VSOs, attorneys) coordinating a veteran's evidence

What to bring

  • Photo ID and any VA correspondence about the exam
  • Service treatment records and current medication list
  • Notes on how the condition affects daily life and work

At a glance

Standard
DBQ-based documentation
Typical length
30–60 minutes per condition
Report turnaround
3–5 business days
We document evidence objectively; ratings are determined by the VA. We'll never promise a specific percentage.
Schedule a C&P exam

Firefighter & First-Responder Physicals

Fitness-for-duty physicals aligned with NFPA 1582, built for the real demands of the job. We help departments keep crews safe and stay compliant — with clear, ready-to-file documentation.

Who it's for

  • Career and volunteer fire departments needing annual physicals
  • EMS, rescue, and public-safety personnel
  • Recruits needing pre-placement or return-to-duty clearance

What it can include

  • Vitals, vision, hearing, and pulmonary screening
  • Cardiac assessment and exposure/cancer-risk screening
  • Department-scheduled group booking available

At a glance

NFPA 1582 guidance
Typical length
45–75 minutes

Scope is tailored to your department's protocol. We'll build a package that fits your standard.
