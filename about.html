Who we are

A clinic built for the people who carry the weight

Patriot Care Center exists for one reason: veterans, firefighters, and the public servants who protect us deserve medical examinations and care that are thorough, unbiased, and delivered with respect. We've been serving them since 2013 — building the practice we wished they had.

Our mission

Get it right, and treat people like it matters

Too often, the exam that decides a veteran's benefits or a firefighter's return to duty is rushed, impersonal, or poorly documented. We set out to change that — pairing rigorous, defensible clinical work with the patience and dignity our patients earned the hard way.

  • Accuracy over speed. The record has to be right, because someone's future depends on it.
  • Independence you can trust. Objective findings, free of any thumb on the scale.
  • Dignity as standard. No one is a number here — not on their best day or their worst.
